Moon+ Vocab Inbox

Moon+ VocabInbox, in Material 3 Expressive

A working prototype, not a picture. The phone responds to touch and to the mouse, so you can judge how it feels, not only how it looks.

Try this

  • touch_appPress and hold a word, then drag up or down. Every word you pass gets selected. Drag near the top or bottom edge and the list scrolls with you.
  • select_allSelect all is the icon at the top right. While selecting, tap a book name to take or drop that whole book.
  • swipeSwipe one word right to send it to Anki, left to discard it. Everything can be undone.
  • downloadTap Import to bring in new marks from a backup and watch them arrive.
  • menu_bookOpen a book in Library to see its cards. Cards already in Anki open too, and saving an edit updates them there.
  • languageIn Settings, open Translate into and search for a language. The list holds about 80 and grows without any change to the screen.
